How it works

From site survey to a running fleet in three steps.

Rollout is phased so the store never closes. Most of the work happens before the first robot arrives.

  1. 01

    Map the store

    We digitize aisles, back-of-house routes, and constraints from your existing store data plus a short on-site survey.

  2. 02

    Deploy in phases

    Robots are configured for your layout and introduced zone by zone, with staff trained as each zone goes live.

  3. 03

    Scale across locations

    The playbook from your first store carries to the next. Each additional site is faster to bring up than the last.
